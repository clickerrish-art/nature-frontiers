## Narration script (approx. 120 words; time to 55 seconds at a measured pace)
“Wildlife videos are strongest when every shot has a purpose. Start with one clear question: what should the viewer discover by the end? Open with your most compelling moment, then give the audience just enough context to understand what they’re seeing. Use close-ups for detail, wider shots to establish the habitat, and clean cuts that keep the story moving. Captions should be easy to read on a phone, and your music should never overpower the narration. Before publishing, check every clip’s license, confirm your facts, and make sure the title matches what the video actually delivers.
